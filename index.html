✦ PULSE90 • ACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE

THE CROWD KNOWS BEFORE YOU DO

PULSE90 was built on a fundamental truth: The stadium crowd anticipates a goal before any camera or computer vision model can. We tap directly into the roar of 80,000 supporters to isolate the moments that mattered.

Scroll to Discover How It Works
✦ STEP 01 • SOUND EXTRACTION

CONTINUOUS AUDIO SCAN

When you upload a broadcast match, the engine strips the video track and extracts a high-fidelity 44.1kHz mono audio stream. Scanning the match acoustically is 100× faster than sluggish frame-by-frame visual analysis.

Scroll to Next Step
✦ STEP 02 • DECIBEL CURVES

RMS ENERGY SPECTROGRAM

Powered by Librosa signal processing, we compute rolling Root Mean Square (RMS) energy windows across 90 minutes. Normal gameplay hum is smoothed out, while crowd surges spike dramatically.

Scroll to Next Step
✦ STEP 03 • STATISTICAL FILTERING

Z-SCORE PEAK DETECTION

Scipy signal algorithms apply adaptive z-score normalization. Commentary voices and whistle sounds are filtered out, isolating the exact timestamps where stadium decibels surge 2× to 4× louder than baseline.

Scroll to Next Step
✦ STEP 04 • FFmpeg PRECISION

SURGICAL REEL CUTTING

FFmpeg buffers 8 seconds of pre-strike buildup and 12 seconds of celebration euphoria around every peak. Clips are sliced with zero re-encoding loss and assembled into your personalized reel in seconds.
